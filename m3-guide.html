⚡ 7-Step Kitchen Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Daily operational sequence for Executive Chef, Sous Chefs, and Ground Station Prep Teams

1
Select 48h Service Period & Influx Window

Select the target date (e.g. 13 Aug / 14 Aug) and meal shift (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner). The system automatically retrieves confirmed in-house guests, expected arrivals, cultural nationalities, and declared diets from Oracle SQL (`RESERVATIONS` table).

2
Review Master Prep Targets & Station Kanban

Filter by kitchen station (🔥 Hot Line & Grill, 🍳 Live Action Counter, 🥗 Cold Pantry & Salads, 🥐 Bakery). Review calculated target weights, unoptimized baselines, food saved in kg, and financial savings in MYR.

3
Execute Staggered 3-Wave Prep Schedule (Just-In-Time Cooking)

Instead of cooking one giant opening batch, switch to the "⏱️ Staggered 3-Wave Timeline" tab. Prep Wave 1 (55%) for buffet opening, Wave 2 (35%) for the peak rush influx, and Wave 3 (10%) for on-demand live top-up.

4
Test What-If Sensitivity Simulations

When unexpected tour bus groups or banquets are scheduled, click "What-If Simulation" and move the In-House Guests, European Influx, or Vegan Pax sliders. The system instantly simulates batch targets and food savings.

5
Recipe BOM Inventory Reconciliation & 1-Click Purchase Requisitions

Click "📦 Recipe BOM & Requisition" to see raw ingredient weights broken down from recommended dishes. If shortages exist in Module 2 Inventory, click "⚡ Auto-Create Purchase Requisition" to generate POs automatically.

6
Log End-of-Shift Plate Returns (EMA Feedback Loop)

At shift conclusion, click "Log Plate Waste". Input discarded table leftovers. If waste was an operational accident (e.g. dropped tray), check "Flag as Operational Accident" so it will NOT unfairly penalize future guest demand multipliers.

7
Print Hardcopy Station Prep Sheets with Scan-to-Log QR Tokens

Click "Print Station Prep Sheet" to print thermal/A4 hardcopy station prep sheets with embedded QR tokens for quick mobile scan-to-log returns on kitchen lines.

📐 Mathematical Formulations & Predictive Models

Algorithmic equations powering the batch optimization calculation

1. Master Dish Prep Target Equation

Target_Prep (kg) = [ (N_guests × C_shift × G_dish × W_demographic × W_dietary × M_EMA_waste) / Y_yield ] + B_safety
• N_guests: Confirmed 48h in-house guests + scheduled arrivals.
• C_shift: Dynamic capture rate (Breakfast = 92-96%, Lunch = 58-70%, Dinner = 76-88%).
• G_dish: Standard portion weight per diner in grams (e.g. 160g for Nasi Lemak, 110g for Salmon).
• W_demographic: Cultural cuisine weight factoring Malaysian/Singaporean vs. European/Western demand.
• W_dietary: Proportional multiplier scaling for Halal, Vegan/Vegetarian, and Gluten-Free declarants.
• M_EMA_waste: Exponential Moving Average decayed plate-waste multiplier ($\alpha = 0.35$).
• Y_yield: Cooking yield and prep trimming loss factor ($0.85$ for roasted meat; $2.5\times$ for rice).
• B_safety: Adaptive buffer margin preventing stockout risk during unexpected service surges.

2. Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Demand Feedback Formula

M_t = α × (1 - Waste_Logged / Batch_Weight) + (1 - α) × M_{t-1} [α = 0.35]

When kitchen ground staff log normal plate returns, the system smooths out historical demand decay. Accidental drops flagged with photo evidence bypass this calculation to preserve model accuracy.
